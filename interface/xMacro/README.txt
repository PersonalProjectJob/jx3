xMacro 1.5 - Huong dan Cai dat & Kich hoat In-Game
=============================================================================

1. CACH KICH HOAT MACRO TRONG GAME:

   Cach 1: Gan phim tat truc tiep (KHUYEN DUNG - 100% Khong loi Font/Engine)
   - Vao game, bam phim ESC -> Chon 'Tùy chọn phím tắt' (Keybindings).
   - Keo xuong muc [xMacro].
   - Gan phim bat ky cho 'xMacro: Thuc thi Macro 1 lan' (vi du: Phim ~ hoac Phim 1).
   - Bam phim da gan de ra combo chieu thuc.

   Cach 2: Dung bang Vi Lenh goc (Thiet lap chi lenh / ESC -> Vi lenh)
   - Mo bang Thiet lap chi lenh goc cua game.
   - Tao 1 Vi Lenh moi, noi dung go DUY NHAT 1 dong:
     /script macro()
     (hoac /macro)
   - Luu va keo bieu tuong macro nay ra thanh ky nang o o so 1.
   - Khi bam o so 1, he thong se goi truc tiep Core xMacro.

   Cach 3: Che do Auto Loop (Tu dong danh lien tuc)
   - Go lenh chat: /xmacro auto
   - Nhan vat se tu dong doc chieu va duy tri DOT, no Ngoc Thach lien tuc.
   - Go lai /xmacro auto de tat.

2. LENH CHAT HO TRO:
   /xver              : Xem phien ban xMacro Core
   /buffnames         : Xem buff tren ban than
   /buffnames target  : Xem debuff tren quai / muc tieu
   /skillname <Ten>   : Tra cuu ID ky nang
   /testcond <DieuKien>: Kiem tra logic dieu kien
